# Contribution Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Fumohouse! Contributions are
welcome to all Fumohouse projects. To avoid any confusion, please read the
following guidelines before you start your contribution.

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to a maintainer.

## Sign-off

Before starting your contribution, please have a maintainer sign off (i.e.,
approve the idea of) your contribution:

- You may be duplicating someone else's work.
- We may warn you that your submission is likely to be rejected.
- We may want to provide advice on the direction your contribution should take
  (e.g., code structure).

When you are signed off to contribute, others will not be signed off for the
same work. You may not reserve jobs in advance; you must be actively working on
anything you reserve. If we do not receive your submission for a long time, we
may ask you about its status. If you do not respond, others may take over.

## Quality

Expect your contribution to undergo several rounds of review/quality control. We
will assist you in this process.

## Integrity

We expect everyone to create, own, and be accountable for their submission. As
such, submitting work created by a ghost author (someone not on the author list
or copyright declaration), otherwise misrepresenting your submission contents,
or using generative AI for your submission contents will not be tolerated.

## Your copyright

You retain the copyright over your work unless it is released into the public
domain. Fumohouse does not claim copyright over your work; it only uses it under
the provided license.

## Credit

For sufficiently large contributions, we will credit you as long as the
contribution is in the game. Small contributions such as typo fixes will not be
credited. You may submit your work under a pen name, which will also be used for
crediting and copyright purposes, or you may request not to be credited at all.

## Retraction by you

We *may* honor requests to remove previous contributions by request on a *best
effort* basis. Note that the open-source/similar licenses Fumohouse uses are
irrevocable and you do not have control over who uses your contribution. Your
contribution will remain permanently and publicly accessible in Git history.

## Retraction by us

We avoid removing contributions as much as possible. However, it may be
necessary to do so in the following situations:

- If there were serious errors in your submission
- If you commit a severe rule violation
- If a rightsholder revokes the license to use the content you submitted

## Copyright declaration

If you use any third-party content (assets, characters, etc.) in your
submission, including those in the public domain, you must provide the following
details:

- Where the content is from
- The copyright owner and/or creator of the content
- The license or guidelines the content is provided under

You must not infringe others' copyright in your submission. If you
believe your usage to be fair use, please note it in your declaration. We may
not accept risky fair use arguments.

## Submission details

Please provide the following details with your submission:

1. Acknowledgment of the license that will be used for each of your submission
   components.
1. [Any third-party copyright details.](#copyright-declaration)

## Process overview

For contributions containing code or scenes, you must submit your contributions
using a pull request (PR) to one of our Git repositories. For contributions
containing assets *only*, you may submit using a PR or via a maintainer.

1. Get your contribution [signed off](./index.md#sign-off).
1. **If you would like to submit via PR:** Fork the relevant Git repository and
   create a feature branch for your submission.
1. Create your contribution. If you need help or have any questions, feel free
   to reach out to us.
1. **If you would like to submit via PR:**
   1. For assets, you must export all assets according to our guidelines and
      provide the original project files (if requested) separately. Do not
      include the project files in your submission.
   1. Submit a pull request on [Forgejo](https://git.seki.pw/Fumohouse) or
      [GitHub](https://github.com/Fumohouse) (if available). If your pull
      request is still a work in progress, submit it as a draft. Drafts will not
      be reviewed unless you request a review from a maintainer or mark it ready
      for review. Include any submission details in code comments or the PR
      description/comments.
1. **If you would like to submit via a maintainer:** Send a maintainer your
   submission details and files.
1. Expect timely feedback on your submission. Address any feedback items and
   resubmit files as necessary.
1. Decision: If your submission is approved, expect to be added to the game in a
   timely manner.

   If your submission will be rejected, we will let you know as soon as
   possible. Depending on the reason for rejection, you may be able to use part
   of your submission in a future one.

Feel free to reach out to us in the event of any unexpected delays.
